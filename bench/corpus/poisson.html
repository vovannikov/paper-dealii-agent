Steady Diffusion with a Volumetric Source and Mixed Boundary Conditions

A. Reviewer and B. Tester

1. Problem description

We study the steady diffusion of a scalar quantity u (for instance a solute concentration) in a rectangular domain Ω=(0,2)×(0,1). The quantity is produced inside the domain at a uniform volumetric rate f, and it is transported by diffusion only; the diffusivity k is constant in space.

2. Governing equation

The balance of the diffusive flux −k∇u against the production rate gives the Poisson equation

−∇·(k∇u)=fin Ω.(1)

3. Boundary conditions

The quantity vanishes on the left and the bottom side of the domain,

u=0on x=0 and on y=0.(2)

Through the right side, x=2, a prescribed outward flux gN leaves the domain,

k∂u∂n=gNon x=2,(3)

where n is the outward unit normal. The top side, y=1, is impermeable, i.e. k∂u/∂n=0 there.

4. Parameters

The diffusivity is k=2.5 and the production rate is f=10. The outward flux through the right side is gN=3. All quantities are given in consistent non-dimensional units.

5. Remarks on the solution

For these data the maximum of u is reached close to the top right corner. Earlier studies with a constant production rate of f=4 and a flux of gN=1 reported a maximum of about 0.9; those values are not used here.
