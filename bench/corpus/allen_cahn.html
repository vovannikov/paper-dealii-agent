Phase Ordering Described by the Allen–Cahn Equation

I. Phase and J. Field

1. Model

The evolution of a non-conserved order parameter c(x,y,t) in the unit square Ω=(0,1)2 is modelled as an L2 gradient flow of the Ginzburg–Landau free energy

F[c]=∫Ω(f(c)+κ2|∇c|2)dΩ,(1)

with the double-well potential

f(c)=14(c2−1)2.(2)

The resulting Allen–Cahn equation reads

∂c∂t=−M(f′(c)−κ∇2c)in Ω,t>0,(3)

where M is the mobility and κ the gradient-energy coefficient.

2. Boundary and initial conditions

No flux crosses the boundary of the domain,

∇c·n=0on ∂Ω,(4)

and the initial state is a smooth saddle-shaped field between the two wells,

c(x,y,0)=12(2x−1)(2y−1).(5)

3. Parameters

We use M=1 and κ=0.01. The simulation runs up to t=1 with the time step Δt=0.01.

4. Discussion

Because the flow is not conservative the mean of c is not preserved, whereas the free energy F decreases monotonically in time.
