Plane-Strain Cantilever Loaded by an End Traction

E. Mechanic and F. Solid

1. Problem description

A short cantilever occupies the rectangle Ω=(0,4)×(0,1) and is analysed in plane strain. The beam is clamped at its left end, x=0. A uniform shear traction acts downwards on the right end, x=4, while the upper and lower faces are free of traction. Body forces are neglected.

2. Governing equations

The displacement field u=(ux,uy) satisfies the equilibrium equation

−∇·σ(u)=fin Ω,(1)

with the body force f. The material is isotropic and linearly elastic, with the stress

σ(u)=λtrε(u)I+2με(u),(2)

and the small-strain tensor

ε(u)=12(∇u+∇uT).(3)

Here λ and μ are the Lamé parameters and I is the identity tensor.

3. Boundary conditions

The clamped end is fixed,

u=0on x=0,(4)

and the prescribed traction on the right end is

σ(u)n=gNon x=4,(5)

with n the outward unit normal. On the faces y=0 and y=1 we have σ(u)n=0.

4. Parameters

The Lamé parameters are λ=1.5 and μ=1.0 (non-dimensional). The body force vanishes, f=(0,0), and the end traction is gN=(0,−0.05).

5. Results

The tip deflection predicted by Euler–Bernoulli theory for this load is about −0.09; the finite element solution is expected to be slightly larger in magnitude because of shear deformation.
