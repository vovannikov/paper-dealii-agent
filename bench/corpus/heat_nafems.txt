2D Stationary Heat Transfer Benchmark
Benchmark HeatTransfer-FEM-Stationary-2D-Single-HeatTransfer-0001
Problem description
This benchmark considers steady-state heat conduction in a rectangular solid. The rectangle
has a width of 0.6 m and a height of 1 m. The lower boundary is maintained at 100 °C. The
upper and right boundaries exchange heat with an ambient environment at 0 °C by natural
convection. The left boundary is thermally insulated.
