1. Problem description
We consider the unsteady heat conduction in a thin square plate Ω= (0, 1)×(0, 1) over the time
interval 0 ≤t ≤1. The plate is initially at the reference temperature T = 0. At t = 0 the left
edge starts to be heated such that its temperature rises linearly with time; the three other edges
are thermally insulated.

4. Parameters
We use κ = 0.5 and a heating rate of β = 2. Time is measured in seconds and temperature
in kelvin above the reference temperature. The computations in Section 5 use the time step
∆t = 0.01.
5. Notes
After the initial transient the interior follows the wall with a constant lag; at t = 1 the temper-
ature at the centre of the plate is of the order of one kelvin.
