2D Stationary Heat Transfer Benchmark

Benchmark HeatTransfer-FEM-Stationary-2D-Single-HeatTransfer-0001

Problem description

This benchmark considers steady-state heat conduction in a rectangular solid. The rectangle has a width of 0.6 m and a height of 1 m. The lower boundary is maintained at 100 °C. The upper and right boundaries exchange heat with an ambient environment at 0 °C by natural convection. The left boundary is thermally insulated.

Governing equation

For steady heat transfer in a solid with no internal heat source and no material velocity, the transient, source, and advection terms vanish. The resulting heat equation is

∇· ( k ∇T ) =0

Here T is temperature and k is the thermal conductivity. Since the material is homogeneous, k=52 W/(m·°C) .

∇· (52∇T) =0 in Ω

Boundary conditions

Bottom boundary

The temperature is prescribed on the lower boundary:

T=100 °C , y=0

Top and right boundaries

These boundaries are subject to Newton's law of cooling with ambient temperature 0 °C and heat-transfer coefficient 750 W/(m²·°C):

−k ∂T ∂n = h (T−Tamb)
h=750 W/(m²·°C) , Tamb=0 °C

The outward normal n is used in the convection condition.

Left boundary

The remaining boundary is thermally insulated, so the normal heat flux vanishes:

−k ∂T ∂n =0 , x=0

Reference solution and verification point

The benchmark uses the temperature at P = (0.6 m, 0.2 m) as the reference quantity. The reference temperature is 18.25 °C.

Reference point P = (0.6 m, 0.2 m)
Reference temperature Tref = 18.25 °C
Verification tolerance relative error < 10−3

The numerical solution is accepted when the relative error at the reference point satisfies

| T(0.6,0.2) −Tref Tref | < 10−3.
