Transient Heating of a Square Plate with a Ramped Wall Temperature

C. Author and D. Engineer

1. Problem description

We consider the unsteady heat conduction in a thin square plate Ω=(0,1)×(0,1) over the time interval 0≤t≤1. The plate is initially at the reference temperature T=0. At t=0 the left edge starts to be heated such that its temperature rises linearly with time; the three other edges are thermally insulated.

2. Governing equation

With the constant thermal diffusivity κ and no internal heat sources, the temperature T(x,y,t) obeys the heat equation

∂T∂t−∇·(κ∇T)=0in Ω,t>0.(1)

3. Initial and boundary conditions

The initial condition is

T(x,y,0)=0.(2)

On the left edge, x=0, the temperature follows the ramp

T(0,y,t)=βt,(3)

where β is the heating rate. The right, bottom and top edges are insulated, so that the normal heat flux vanishes there:

κ∂T∂n=0on x=1,y=0,y=1.(4)

4. Parameters

We use κ=0.5 and a heating rate of β=2. Time is measured in seconds and temperature in kelvin above the reference temperature. The computations in Section 5 use the time step Δt=0.01.

5. Notes

After the initial transient the interior follows the wall with a constant lag; at t=1 the temperature at the centre of the plate is of the order of one kelvin.
